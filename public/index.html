The scenario

A real onboarding call, played live.

Cartwell, a fictional online marketplace, is nine days into a 14-day Arize trial. Their engineering lead booked a 45-minute working session through Calendly to get tracing running before the holiday code freeze.

Grace Liu, Cartwell’s engineering lead, is an AI customer. A Gemini Live voice and a Spatius avatar, joined over LiveKit. She asks real onboarding questions and believes whatever you tell her.

You are Jordan

Who it’s for

Technical onboarding CSMs, live on 10 to 20 customer calls a week.

Customer success managers and solutions consultants at B2B software companies. Every call is a live product exam in front of a customer, usually with the same accounts week after week. They book those calls with Calendly today; they’re Calendly’s core Teams and Enterprise users.

The MVP

Four capabilities, in priority order. Everything else waits.

Scoped for one PM, four engineers and one designer to ship a public beta in under 12 weeks.

  1. Answers. The customer asks; an answer card with sources lands in 3 to 4 seconds, drawn from the company’s docs and the account notes.
  2. Fact-checks. The CSM misspeaks; a red correction blinks with a “say instead” line. A customer misconception gets a private Clarify card.
  3. Show and send. Callie draws flows, code and checklists on a whiteboard the CSM can present, and drafts emails and one-page guides sent with one click.
  4. Coaching. Private nudges when the customer sounds confused, skeptical or frustrated, or when the CSM is talking too much.

Out of scope for the MVP

  • Speaking to the room
  • Sending anything without a click
  • Post-meeting follow-ups (Notetaker and Callie already cover them)
  • CRM writes
  • Non-English calls
  • Internal meetings

Effortless, then trusted

Zero setup in the moment. A little more autonomy every week.

Zero setup

Context loads from the Calendly booking: who’s coming, why, in the invitee’s own words, and what was promised last time. Listening starts with the call. No bot joins the meeting.

One moment at a time

A single Now card with the line to say, readable in two seconds. A whiteboard that draws itself. A one-line history. Details sit behind “More.”

Audience-aware

Internal notes such as “trial ends in 5 days” never reach anything the customer can see. Present opens a customer-safe whiteboard without corrections or private notes.

Quality

Correct, timely and appropriate, measured before launch.

A confidently wrong answer or a false correction is a Sev-1. Launch requires zero Sev-1s in the last 200 eval items.

DimensionWhat “good” meansHow we measure it
Correct and groundedEvery claim cites a sourcePrecision on a gold set of 300 real call questions; Sev-1 count
TimelyArrives before the moment passesAnswer p50 under 3 s, p90 under 5 s; whisper starts under 1 s after a correction
AppropriateInterrupts only when it mattersAt least 80% of corrections accepted, not dismissed; coaching rated helpful

Every call line is a test case

Each scripted line has an expected outcome: answer, correction, clarify or coach. The suite reruns on every prompt or model change. This prototype’s 13-chapter recorded call has 15 checks, and all of them pass.

A weekly taste review

Design and engineering read 30 real cards together each week: was it right, was it needed, was it on time, was it kind? The answers tune prompts and interruption thresholds.

Metrics

North star: in-call resolution rate.

The share of customer questions answered during the call, with no “I’ll get back to you.” Baseline it before launch from existing Notetaker transcripts by counting questions and the deferrals that answer them later.

From now to launch

Twelve weeks, four phases, one live demo every week.

  1. Weeks 0–2

    Discovery and evals

    Interview 15 CSMs, including Calendly’s own CS team. Shadow 10 calls. Collect 300 real questions as the gold set. Define Sev-1. Sign 3 design partners.

  2. Weeks 3–6

    Core loop

    Desktop capture, answers, fact-checks, the whiteboard and one-click send. Dogfood on Calendly’s own onboarding calls.

  3. Weeks 7–9

    Private beta

    Three to five design-partner companies, each with their own docs as the fact base. Weekly quality reviews. Tune interruptions.

  4. Weeks 10–12

    Public beta in Teams Plus

    Launch gates: zero Sev-1, p90 under 5 seconds, at least 80% correction acceptance. Launch story, enablement and help docs.

Risks

The top execution and adoption risks, and what I’d do about each.

RiskMitigation
A confidently wrong answerGrounded-only answers with citations and confidence labels; an “I’d check X” fallback; the Sev-1 launch gate. Answers go to the CSM, never straight to the customer.
Interruption fatigueSilent by default; blink only for serious corrections; the whisper is opt-in; coaching is rate-limited.
Recording consent and privacyDisclosure at booking and in the meeting, the way Notetaker already notifies invitees; no audio stored by default; admin controls for Enterprise.
A stale knowledge baseScheduled doc ingestion; the source and its date on every card.
Zoom, Meet and Teams build it themselvesBe the CSM’s assistant across every platform, with Calendly’s booking context and actions the platforms don’t have.
CSMs fear being watchedPosition it as a private coach, not monitoring. Managers never see individual coaching.

Strategies I’d reuse

Evals first, demos before decks.

  • Build the eval before the feature. In this prototype, every demo line doubles as a test case.
  • Ship a working demo before the deck. This page and the live call are the pitch.
  • Dogfood first. Weeks 3 to 6 run on Calendly’s own onboarding calls before any customer sees it.

Not locked to one platform

Today’s in-meeting AI is tied to one platform: Teams Facilitator, Zoom’s assistant, Google’s Ask Gemini. Each works for the meeting owner’s company. Callie Live is the CSM’s own assistant, on any platform, grounded in their company’s knowledge.

How the prototype works

Two audio channels: the customer and the CSM. Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite hears each utterance and its tone in about a second; Gemini 3.8 Flash answers and fact-checks from hybrid retrieval over the docs; Gemini Live whispers in under a second. The real app listens at the computer level, with no bot in the meeting.
