Calendly Labs · AI Assistant case

Never say “I’ll get back to you” again.

Callie Live is a private copilot for customer calls. It listens from your laptop, answers the customer’s question before you finish saying “good question,” catches your mistakes before they build on them, and puts the right diagram or doc on screen while you’re still talking.

Priority 2

Fact-checks

When the CSM states something wrong, a red correction blinks within a few seconds with a graceful line to say instead. When the customer has it wrong, Callie hands the CSM a private Clarify card. Serious corrections can also be whispered in the CSM’s ear.

Priority 3

Coaching

Callie reads the customer’s tone from her voice and words. When she sounds confused, skeptical or frustrated, or when the CSM has talked too long, a private nudge suggests the next move. It’s rate-limited, and it’s never visible to the customer or reported to a manager.

Zero setup in the moment. A little more autonomy every week.

Integration and setup

Set up once by an admin. Nothing to do before each call.

Callie Live is a desktop app that signs in with Calendly. It hears the call at the computer level, so it works next to Zoom, Meet or Teams without a bot.

  1. 1

    Install and sign in

    Mac or Windows app, Calendly single sign-on. The admin turns it on for the CS team in Calendly’s admin settings.

  2. 2

    Connect the knowledge

    Point it at the docs site, help center, and Notion, Confluence or Google Drive. Callie indexes them and refreshes nightly.

  3. 3

    Review the key facts

    An admin confirms a short list of facts that must never be wrong: plans, limits, compliance. Fact-checks lean on these first.

  4. 4

    Connect email

    Gmail or Outlook, so “Send” goes out from the CSM’s own address after their click.

  5. 5

    Booking context flows in

    Event type, invitee answers, notes and earlier Notetaker recaps arrive with each call. Nothing to paste.

  6. 6

    Consent built in

    A disclosure line in the booking confirmation, no audio stored by default, and admin controls for retention and who can turn it on.

Quality

Judged feature by feature, before launch and every week after.

A confidently wrong answer or a false correction is a Sev-1. Launch requires zero Sev-1s in the last 200 eval items.

FeatureWhat good looks likeHow we measure itLaunch gate
Answers: say itCorrect, grounded, short enough to sayPrecision on a gold set of 300 real call questions; citation coverage; in-call resolutionZero Sev-1; p50 under 3 s, p90 under 5 s
Answers: show itThe visual matches what the CSM is explainingWeekly taste review; how often visuals get presentedNo invented package names or limits
Answers: send itThe customer can follow it aloneShare of drafts sent with light or no editsEvery step and link traceable to a source
Fact-checksCatches real errors, never invents oneRecall on seeded mistakes; false-correction rate; correction acceptanceAt least 80% accepted; zero false corrections in the gate set
CoachingRight moment, right tone, rarelyHelpful ratings; dismiss rate; nudges per callRated helpful by most design-partner CSMs

Every call line is a test case

Each scripted line has an expected outcome: answer, correction, clarify or coach. The suite reruns on every prompt or model change. This prototype’s recorded call has 15 checks and all of them pass, and the live call has its own end-to-end test with a simulated microphone.

A weekly taste review

Design and engineering read 30 real cards together each week. Was it right, was it needed, was it on time, was it kind? The answers tune prompts and interruption thresholds.

Metrics

North star: in-call resolution rate.

The share of customer questions answered during the call, with no “I’ll get back to you.” Baseline it before launch from existing Notetaker transcripts by counting questions and the deferrals that answer them later.

From now to launch

Demo, build, beta, public beta.

  1. Weeks 0–2

    Demo it to CSMs

    Put this prototype in front of 5 to 15 CSMs, including Calendly’s own CS team. Collect 300 real questions for the gold set, define Sev-1, and sign three design partners.

  2. Weeks 3–6

    Build in a weekly loop

    Build, run the evals, dogfood on Calendly’s own onboarding calls, repeat. Desktop capture, answers, fact-checks and one-click send come first.

  3. Weeks 7–9

    Private beta

    Three to five design partners, each with their own docs as the fact base. Weekly quality reviews and tuning of interruptions.

  4. Weeks 10–12

    Public beta

    In Teams Plus, behind the launch gates: zero Sev-1, p90 under 5 seconds, at least 80% correction acceptance.

The context nobody else has

The booking says who’s coming, why and what was promised. Platform assistants in Zoom, Teams and Meet don’t see it, and they work for the meeting owner’s company, not the CSM.

Revenue where Calendly is strongest

Customer-facing teams already run on Calendly Teams and Enterprise. A live copilot is a reason to add seats and upgrade, measured by Teams Plus attach.

A loop that compounds

Every question asked live shows what the docs are missing and grows the eval set, so answers get better as more calls run through it.

How it relates to Notetaker

Notetaker remembers the meeting. Callie Live helps you during it.

  • Shared capture. Both hear the call; Calendly is already testing bot-free capture for Notetaker, and Callie Live rides the same direction.
  • A clean handoff. Answers, corrections and sends from the call flow into the Notetaker recap, so the follow-up is mostly done.
  • The baseline. Notetaker transcripts measure today’s in-call resolution rate and seed the eval set before launch.
  • One Callie. Before, during and after the meeting, under one assistant brand.
